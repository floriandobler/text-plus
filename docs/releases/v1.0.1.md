Bug fix release for **Text+** 1.0.

**Fixed**
- Flicker and jagged notches in merged outlines when *Opacity* and *Offset* are animated together and characters or lines slide over each other. With many overlapping glyphs the renderer could drop a relevant glyph from its per-pixel selection; the selection now follows the opacity order and skips glyphs out of reach of every effect. Scenes without such overlaps render exactly as in 1.0.0.

**Install / update**
1. Unzip and replace `TextPlus.bundle` in `~/Documents/Resolume Arena/Extra Effects` (or Resolume Avenue).
2. Remove the download quarantine once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Documents/"Resolume Arena"/"Extra Effects"/TextPlus.bundle`
3. Restart Resolume. Existing compositions keep their settings.

Universal binary (Apple Silicon + Intel), macOS 11 or later.
